01. Introduction to Medical Terminology

Definition

Medical terminology is a system of words used by healthcare professionals to communicate accurately about health, diseases, body structures, medical conditions and patient care.

Importance of Medical Terminology

  • Helps healthcare professionals communicate clearly.
  • Improves understanding of medical records.
  • Helps nurses understand diagnoses and treatment plans.
  • Reduces communication errors.
  • Supports accurate documentation and reporting.

02. Basic Word Components

Prefix

A word part placed at the beginning of a medical term.

Example: Hyper- means excessive or above normal.

Root Word

The main part that gives the basic meaning of a medical term.

Example: Cardi means heart.

Suffix

A word part placed at the end of a medical term.

Example: -itis means inflammation.

Combining Vowel

A vowel, usually 'o', used to connect word parts.

04. Common Medical Suffixes

  • -itis : Inflammation
  • -ectomy : Surgical removal
  • -ology : Study of
  • -algia : Pain
  • -emia : Blood condition
  • -scopy : Visual examination
  • -gram : Record or image
  • -megaly : Enlargement
  • -oma : Tumour or mass
  • -pathy : Disease

05. Common Medical Root Words

  • Cardi : Heart
  • Nephr : Kidney
  • Hepat : Liver
  • Gastr : Stomach
  • Derm : Skin
  • Oste : Bone
  • Neur : Nerve
  • Haemat : Blood
  • Rhin : Nose
  • Ophthalm : Eye

06. Examples of Medical Terms

  • Cardiology – Study of the heart.
  • Dermatitis – Inflammation of the skin.
  • Nephrology – Study of the kidneys.
  • Tachycardia – Abnormally fast heart rate.
  • Bradycardia – Abnormally slow heart rate.
  • Gastrectomy – Surgical removal of all or part of the stomach.

07. Nursing Importance

  • Understand doctors' instructions.
  • Read and interpret patient records.
  • Document nursing care accurately.
  • Communicate effectively with healthcare teams.
  • Understand investigations and treatment procedures.

Quick Revision

  • Prefix = Beginning of a word.
  • Root = Main meaning.
  • Suffix = Ending of a word.
  • Cardi = Heart.
  • -itis = Inflammation.
  • -ectomy = Surgical removal.
