Medical and Educational Disclaimer

This book is an educational quick-reference resource. It is not a diagnostic tool, treatment protocol, prescription guide or substitute for a qualified clinician, current institutional policy, laboratory report or specialist advice.

Reference intervals vary with age, sex, pregnancy, altitude, population, specimen, collection method, equipment and laboratory assay. Use the interval printed by the performing laboratory for individual results.

A value inside a reference interval does not always mean a person is healthy; a value outside it does not alone establish a diagnosis. Never delay urgent care or change treatment based only on this book. Students and staff must follow training, scope of practice, supervision and local protocols.

How to Use This Book

Unless stated otherwise, laboratory ranges are typical adult reference values. Pediatric, neonatal, pregnancy and older-adult values may differ substantially. Units are shown beside values. Verify the local laboratory interval before clinical interpretation.
